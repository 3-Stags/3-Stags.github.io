🎉 It's out. **Adventurous** is approved, released, and available on the App Store today.

Back in June [we said we were getting close](/posts/adventurous-coming-soon). Since then it's been through TestFlight, a lot of polish, and Apple's review. Now anyone with an iPhone can download it.

{{< appstore >}}

## What it does

Adventurous gives you one fresh adventure location every single day — different each time, calibrated to your schedule, and ready to go when you are. Not a vacation. Not a road trip. Just a reason to step outside your usual routine and discover something nearby you wouldn't have found on your own.

- 🗓️ **A new adventure every day** — no two days the same
- 🎯 **Personalized to your life** — it respects your work days and your days off
- 📍 **Local and nearby** — scaled to how far you can realistically go
- 🌤️ **Points of interest and weather** — everything you need to make the call
- ⏰ **Daily reminders** — a nudge as early as 7 AM, plus a prompt to rate where you went
- 🔒 **Private by design** — your location and history stay on your device

## 💵 What it costs

Every new account starts with a **free 4-day trial**. After that it's **$6.99 weekly**, **$59.99 a year**, or **$119.99 a year for the whole family** via Family Sharing.

## 🔒 Privacy

Your location is used to find adventures near you, and your adventure history lives on your device. The full details are in the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
